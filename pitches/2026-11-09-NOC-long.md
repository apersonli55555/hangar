## Thesis

Northrop Grumman's revenue grew from $41.03 billion in 2024
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1133421/000113342125000006/0001133421-25-000006-index.htm))
to $41.95 billion in 2025
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1133421/000113342126000003/0001133421-26-000003-index.htm)),
about 2%, the slowest among the large defense primes with 2025 figures on Hangar
(L3Harris's 2025 revenue is not available in its XBRL data).

Its remaining performance obligations (RPO) rose from $95.7 billion at December 31, 2025
(same 10-K) to $109.6 billion at September 30, 2026
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1133421/000113342126000038/0001133421-26-000038-index.htm)),
up 15% in nine months. RPO is about 2.6 times 2025 revenue.

My view: backlog growing this much faster than revenue should lift revenue growth above 2025's 2%.

## Evidence

- From April to September 2026, Hangar counts $5.5 billion of definite awards to Northrop
  Grumman in Department of War announcements; most of the RPO increase came from elsewhere
  (classified work is not announced, and RPO includes non-defense customers).

## Catalysts

- The 2026 10-K and the 2027 quarterly reports.

## Risks / what would prove me wrong

- A large share of Northrop's work is classified, so outsiders can't see what drives the backlog.
- Fixed-price development programs can lose money if costs run over.
- I'm wrong if revenue for the four quarters ending September 2027 grows 3% or less compared
  with the four quarters before.

## Valuation

No view on valuation here: this is a call on revenue growth, and Hangar shows no prices.
A student's opinion, not investment advice.

$46,210 million and Northrop keeps its 2.0x EV/sales, enterprise value reaches about $91,200
million; subtracting $15,197 million debt, adding $3,744 million cash and dividing by 142.1 million
shares gives $561.35. I keep the multiple flat, so the target comes only from revenue growth.

This is the weakest of my long targets: 7% is not much more than the market might return anyway.

**What changes the call:** I'm wrong on the stock if it is below $525.01 in November 2027, or if it
trails SPY's return over the same 12 months.
