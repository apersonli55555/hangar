## Thesis

Huntington Ingalls builds the Navy's aircraft carriers, submarines and amphibious ships.
Its remaining performance obligations (RPO), the contracted work it has not yet delivered,
rose from $57.3 billion at June 30, 2026
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000047/0001501585-26-000047-index.htm))
to $86.2 billion at September 30, 2026
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000056/0001501585-26-000056-index.htm)).
That is a $29 billion increase in one quarter.

Revenue has grown slowly: from $8.9 billion in 2019
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158520000011/0001501585-20-000011-index.htm))
to $12.5 billion in 2025
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000006/0001501585-26-000006-index.htm)),
about 6% a year. RPO is now about 6.9 times 2025 revenue.

My view: a backlog this large, this suddenly, should lift revenue growth above its old
6% pace over the next year, as work on the new contracts starts.

## Evidence

- RPO grew by $29 billion in the third quarter of 2026.
- Department of War announcements that Hangar matched to HII in the same quarter add up to
  $5.8 billion of definite awards, the largest a $5.1 billion contract for the USS Harry S. Truman
  ([Sept. 28, 2026 announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20261019223014/https://www.war.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/4614322/contracts-for-sept-28-2026/)).
  The rest of the increase came from contracts that were not in those daily announcements;
  I have not yet identified them.

## Catalysts

- The 2026 10-K and the 2027 quarterly reports: does revenue growth move above 6%?
- Whether RPO stays near its new level instead of falling back.

## Risks / what would prove me wrong

- Shipyards may not have the workers or suppliers to build faster, so the backlog turns into
  revenue slowly.
- Fixed-price and fixed-ceiling contracts mean cost overruns reduce profit.
- I'm wrong if revenue for the four quarters ending September 30, 2027 grows 6% or less
  compared with the four quarters before, or if RPO falls back below $70 billion by then.

## Valuation

I have no view on valuation here: this pitch is a call on revenue growth, and Hangar shows
no prices. It is a student's opinion, not investment advice.

## Update 2026-11-09

Adding the price side of the pitch, which the original left out.

**Entry price: $288.74**, HII's closing price on Friday, November 6, 2026, the last close before this pitch was published (looked up on Yahoo Finance). SPY (an S&P 500 fund) closed at $735.29 that day, so the pitch can be scored against the market.

**Valuation: enterprise value to sales (EV/sales).** Enterprise value is the share price times shares outstanding, plus debt, minus cash. Prices are Friday, November 6, 2026 closes from Yahoo Finance; everything else is from each company's latest SEC filing.

- **HII: 1.0x.** $288.74 × 39.4 million shares, $2,704 million debt, $387 million cash; revenue $13,530 million over the four quarters to September 30, 2026 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000056/0001501585-26-000056-index.htm), [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000006/0001501585-26-000006-index.htm)).
- **General Dynamics (GD): 1.7x.** $354.06 × 270.2 million shares, $7,016 million debt, $4,388 million cash; revenue $56,520 million over the four quarters to October 4, 2026 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40533/000004053326000038/0000040533-26-000038-index.htm), [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40533/000004053326000006/0000040533-26-000006-index.htm)).
- **BWX Technologies (BWXT): 3.4x.** $119.52 × 91.6 million shares, $2,022 million debt, $569 million cash; revenue $3,601 million over the four quarters to September 30, 2026 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486957/000148695726000051/0001486957-26-000051-index.htm), [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486957/000148695726000007/0001486957-26-000007-index.htm)).

So HII is valued at about one times sales, the lowest of the three, while its backlog is about
6.9 years of revenue.

**12-month price target: $323 (up 12%).** If revenue over the next four quarters grows 10% to
about $14,880 million and HII keeps its 1.0x EV/sales, enterprise value reaches about $15,060
million; subtracting $2,704 million debt, adding $387 million cash and dividing by 39.4 million
shares gives $323.49. I keep the multiple flat, so the target comes only from revenue growth.

**What changes the call:** I'm wrong on the stock if it is below $288.74 in November 2027, or if it
trails SPY's return over the same 12 months.

## Update 2026-11-10

Adding a second valuation check (EV/EBITDA) and HII's own guidance.

**How EV/EBITDA is calculated here.** EBITDA is operating income plus depreciation and amortization, both over the last four reported quarters, taken from each company's SEC filings (the full fiscal year, plus this year so far, minus the same months a year earlier). It is my calculation, not a figure the companies report. Enterprise value is the same one used in the EV/sales comparison above, at the November 6 closing prices.

- **HII: 12.4x.** EBITDA $1,100 million: operating income $783 million plus D&A $317 million, over the four quarters to September 30, 2026 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000056/0001501585-26-000056-index.htm), [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000006/0001501585-26-000006-index.htm)).
- **General Dynamics (GD): 14.4x.** EBITDA $6,809 million: operating income $5,859 million plus D&A $950 million, to October 4, 2026 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40533/000004053326000038/0000040533-26-000038-index.htm), [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/40533/000004053326000006/0000040533-26-000006-index.htm)).
- **BWX Technologies (BWXT): 21.8x.** EBITDA $568 million: operating income $445 million plus D&A $123 million, to September 30, 2026 ([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486957/000148695726000051/0001486957-26-000051-index.htm), [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1486957/000148695726000007/0001486957-26-000007-index.htm)).

HII is the cheapest of the three on EBITDA as well as on sales. As a cross-check (my calculation): at General Dynamics' 14.4x, HII's current EBITDA gives an enterprise value of about $15,840 million, or about **$343 a share** after debt and cash, above my $323 target.

**Guidance.** In its [third-quarter 2026 results](https://hii.com/news/hii-reports-third-quarter-2026-results) on October 29 ([8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000054/0001501585-26-000054-index.htm)), HII raised its 2026 shipbuilding revenue range to $10.5 to $10.7 billion (was $10.2 to $10.4 billion), reaffirmed a shipbuilding operating margin of 6.0% to 6.5% and its 2026 free cash flow outlook, and kept Mission Technologies revenue at $3.0 to $3.2 billion.

**What this means for the target.** Higher shipbuilding guidance fits the thesis that the backlog is starting to turn into revenue. The EBITDA check suggests my $323 target, which comes only from revenue growth at a flat multiple, is conservative. I'm keeping it.
