## Thesis

Huntington Ingalls builds the Navy's aircraft carriers, submarines and amphibious ships.
Its remaining performance obligations (RPO), the contracted work it has not yet delivered,
rose from $57.3 billion at June 30, 2026
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000047/0001501585-26-000047-index.htm))
to $86.2 billion at September 30, 2026
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000056/0001501585-26-000056-index.htm)).
That is a $29 billion increase in one quarter.

Revenue has grown slowly: from $8.9 billion in 2019
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158520000011/0001501585-20-000011-index.htm))
to $12.5 billion in 2025
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1501585/000150158526000006/0001501585-26-000006-index.htm)),
about 6% a year. RPO is now about 6.9 times 2025 revenue.

My view: a backlog this large, this suddenly, should lift revenue growth above its old
6% pace over the next year, as work on the new contracts starts.

## Evidence

- RPO grew by $29 billion in the third quarter of 2026.
- Department of War announcements that Hangar matched to HII in the same quarter add up to
  $5.8 billion of definite awards, the largest a $5.1 billion contract for the USS Harry S. Truman
  ([Sept. 28, 2026 announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20261019223014/https://www.war.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/4614322/contracts-for-sept-28-2026/)).
  The rest of the increase came from contracts that were not in those daily announcements;
  I have not yet identified them.

## Catalysts

- The 2026 10-K and the 2027 quarterly reports: does revenue growth move above 6%?
- Whether RPO stays near its new level instead of falling back.

## Risks / what would prove me wrong

- Shipyards may not have the workers or suppliers to build faster, so the backlog turns into
  revenue slowly.
- Fixed-price and fixed-ceiling contracts mean cost overruns reduce profit.
- I'm wrong if revenue for the four quarters ending September 30, 2027 grows 6% or less
  compared with the four quarters before, or if RPO falls back below $70 billion by then.

## Valuation

I have no view on valuation here: this pitch is a call on revenue growth, and Hangar shows
no prices. It is a student's opinion, not investment advice.
