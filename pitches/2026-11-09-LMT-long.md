## Thesis

Lockheed Martin's remaining performance obligations (RPO) rose from $179.1 billion at
September 28, 2025
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/936468/000162828025045693/0001628280-25-045693-index.htm))
to $292.0 billion at September 27, 2026
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/936468/000162828026066928/0001628280-26-066928-index.htm)),
up 63%. Most of the increase came in two quarters: RPO was $230.4 billion at June 28, 2026
([10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/936468/000162828026049411/0001628280-26-049411-index.htm)).

Revenue grew from $71.0 billion in 2024
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/936468/000093646825000009/0000936468-25-000009-index.htm))
to $75.0 billion in 2025
([10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/936468/000162828026004195/0001628280-26-004195-index.htm)),
about 6%. RPO is now about 3.9 times 2025 revenue.

My view: backlog growing ten times faster than revenue should lift revenue growth above 6%.

## Evidence

- From April to September 2026, Hangar counts $32.4 billion of definite awards to Lockheed
  Martin in Department of War announcements.
- Two more single awards in that period, $35.3 billion on June 24
  ([announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20260714155829/https://www.war.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/4525817/contracts-for-june-24-2026/))
  and $53.9 billion on July 30
  ([announcement](https://web.archive.org/web/20260805110841/https://www.war.gov/News/Contracts/Contract/Article/4560267/contracts-for-july-30-2026/)),
  are held out of Hangar's totals until a person confirms them against the source, as every
  amount over $10 billion is.

## Catalysts

- The 2026 10-K and the 2027 quarterly reports: does revenue growth move above 6%?

## Risks / what would prove me wrong

- Multiyear contracts are delivered over many years, so a large backlog can turn into revenue slowly.
- Fixed-price programs can lose money if costs run over.
- I'm wrong if revenue for the four quarters ending September 2027 grows 6% or less compared
  with the four quarters before.
